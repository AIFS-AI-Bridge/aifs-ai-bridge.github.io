Our Mission

Bridging the Gap Between AI and Industry

Artificial intelligence is reshaping every discipline it touches — but most introductions to it are written for people who already write software for a living.

AIBridge exists to close that gap. We teach the working ideas behind machine learning to students, graduates, and professionals from non-computer-science backgrounds — so they can apply AI to the field they already know best, whether that's agriculture, medicine, finance, design, or the classroom.

Every camp is taught by volunteers, is free to attend, and pairs a short lecture with a hands-on lab you run yourself in the browser. No installs. No prerequisites beyond curiosity.

  • Fundamentals that stick Python, data exploration, and the core ML algorithms — taught from intuition first, notation second.
  • A working toolbox pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib, and Google Colab — the same stack practitioners actually reach for.
  • Room to keep going Every lab ships at two levels — guided and unscaffolded — plus a solution key you keep for good.
  • A shared vocabulary So you can brief an ML team, read a paper, or scope a project without needing a translator.
What We Teach

Nine lectures, eight labs, one throughline

The curriculum runs from a first line of Python to the transformers behind modern chatbots — two lectures and their labs a day, across five days.

Python, from zero

Input and output, lists, functions, imports. Enough language to be dangerous with data — written and run in Colab, so a browser is the only requirement.

Machine learning that works

Regression, decision trees, random forests, SVMs, k-means, PCA — plus the parts people skip: what accuracy hides, why models overfit, and when data is simply bad.

LLMs and generative AI

What large language models can and can't do, how they got here, and how to reason about them honestly — the questions everyone is being asked at work right now.
